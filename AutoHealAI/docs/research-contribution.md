# Research Contribution

## Novelty

- Integrates predictive maintenance with autonomous cloud remediation loop.
- Couples model confidence with explainable playbook selection.
- Adds deployment risk analysis as a post-release safety mechanism.

## Evaluation Suggestions

- Precision/Recall for failure prediction.
- Mean time to detect (MTTD) and mean time to recover (MTTR).
- False remediation rate and rollback effectiveness.
